# Roadmap: Aurora v1.17 Oracle Scheduler Reliability

**Status:** Complete ✅
**Milestone:** v1.17 Oracle Scheduler Reliability
**Started:** 2026-08-23

## Overview

v1.15's scheduler let a persistently-failing source be retried every ~1s
forever, hammering a broken endpoint. v1.17 makes the scheduler resilient to
outages by backing off exponentially (and cap) on repeated failures.

## Success Criteria

- [x] Suppress re-fetching a source during a capped exponential backoff window
      after failures (1s, 2s, 4s, ... max 5m); a success resets the streak.
- [x] Tests: backoff escalation + cap, failed-source suppression during the
      window, retry after the window elapses.
