**Status:** In progress
**Milestone:** v1.6 Interactive Surface Parity

## Overview

Make every documented interactive surface actually work. An audit of
`cmd/aurora/cmd` against AGENTS.md/README found that `token tui` was the only
`tui` subcommand left as a stubbed placeholder ("TUI not implemented yet")
even though the full Bubble Tea implementation (`internal/ui/token`'s
`RunTokenTUI`) already existed. Lottery, NFT and Oracle wire their real TUIs;
token did not.

Verifying the milestone surfaced two further genuine, pre-existing issues that
were fixed as part of the loop's VERIFY gate.
